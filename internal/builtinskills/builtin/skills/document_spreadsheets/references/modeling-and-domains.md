# Conditional domain checks

Apply only the section needed for the user's task. Preserve source conventions and avoid expanding a tracker into a full model.

## Financial models

Join source schedules by period/date keys, avoid overlapping actual and forecast periods, and preserve historical values. Distinguish recognition from cash timing. Keep headcount, FTE and paid FTE separate; state salary units and prorate supplied start/end dates consistently.

For asset schedules, reconcile opening balance, additions, depreciation, disposals and closing balance. Respect in-service dates, remaining useful life and the requested depreciation method. For integrated statements, reconcile cash and retained earnings; do not use unexplained plugs to conceal a mismatch.

Show variance amounts and percentages with explicit denominator and favorable/unfavorable direction. Keep valuation method, reporting date and assumptions consistent. Reconcile enterprise value to equity value where relevant, and verify IRR cash-flow signs and timing independently.

Use the main skill's working-area formula colors while keeping headline outputs dark. Scenario and sensitivity outputs must use the same underlying model, with captured comparisons clearly distinguished from live calculations.

## Healthcare

Preserve raw records and record corrections separately. Retain supplied unit/code definitions and legends. Use thresholds from the provided protocol or an identified authoritative source, never invented limits. Cross-check critical calculations against that source or qualified review and state any unresolved validation limitation.

## Marketing

Distinguish data as-of dates from analysis time. Preserve raw and cleaned data with traceable mappings and exclusions. Use consistent attribution windows and denominators. Keep source-provided targets and whether higher/lower is better explicit; do not invent benchmarks. Retain typed timestamps unless a text export requires an additional representation.

## Research

Keep one variable per column and one observation per row, with units and explicit typed exclusion flags rather than color-only meanings. Preserve raw observations. Record transformations, uncertainty and simulation assumptions; use a reproducible seed/method for simulated data and label it as simulated.
