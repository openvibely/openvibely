The Duck Who Followed a Star

Every evening, a little duck named Pip watched a bright star appear above the pond. While the other ducks tucked their beaks beneath their wings, Pip wondered where its reflection went when the water rippled.

One night, he decided to find out.

He paddled toward the silver sparkle, but it slipped away with every stroke. He went past the reeds, around the old willow, and straight into a sleeping frog.

“Sorry,” Pip whispered. “I'm trying to catch a star.”

The frog blinked. “Have you tried sitting still?”

Pip had tried paddling faster. He had tried sneaking up on it. Sitting still seemed unlikely, but his feet were tired.

So he floated beside the frog.

Slowly, the ripples faded. The bright reflection returned, resting just in front of his beak.

“Oh,” said Pip.

He stayed there a long time, watching the star tremble whenever he breathed. Then a cool breeze stirred the willow, and he remembered his warm nest on the bank.

“Will it be here tomorrow?” he asked.

“Probably,” said the frog. “I usually am.”

The next evening, Pip brought a friend. They floated quietly beside the willow until the star appeared.

Pip still wanted to know how it got into the pond. But for that evening, he was happy to show someone where to look.
